GoStay — Terms & Conditions

Draft version 1.0 — for legal review before launch.

1. What GoStay is

GoStay is designed as an online accommodation marketplace. Unless a separate agreement says otherwise, a host provides the accommodation and remains responsible for the property, its operation and its legal obligations. GoStay is not automatically the owner, landlord, hotel operator or insurer of a listed property.

2. Eligibility and truthful information

  • Users must provide truthful information and use only their own account.
  • Users must not impersonate another person or provide false documents.
  • GoStay may restrict accounts where identity, authority, safety or fraud concerns cannot reasonably be resolved.

3. Listings

  • Hosts must own or be authorised to list the accommodation.
  • Descriptions, photos, prices, amenities, location, occupancy and rules must be accurate.
  • A GoStay listing or badge does not itself replace a government licence, permit, inspection or insurance requirement.

4. Bookings

Guests must provide accurate dates and party size. Hosts must not knowingly accept a booking they cannot honour. Total price, fees, deposit, cancellation terms and material restrictions should be shown before a guest commits.

5. Payments

Production payments should use an approved payment provider and business settlement arrangement. GoStay should never ask users for card PINs, mobile-money PINs or passwords. Payment status should be confirmed by secure server-side callbacks rather than screenshots or unverified messages.

6. Private rooms and shared homes

  • Hosts must disclose shared spaces and who may be present.
  • Guests must respect privacy, quiet hours and house rules.
  • Guests must not enter private host areas without permission.
  • Hosts must not secretly record or monitor guests.

7. Safety and prohibited conduct

  • No theft, fraud, violence, threats, harassment, property damage, unlawful activity or dangerous behaviour.
  • No parties or events where prohibited by the listing or host rules.
  • No weapons or illegal drugs where prohibited by law or property rules.
  • No trafficking, exploitation or other unlawful use of a GoStay property.

8. Reviews

Reviews must reflect genuine experiences. Bribery, threats, extortion, private information, hate, harassment and knowingly false reviews are prohibited.

9. Enforcement

GoStay may pause listings, restrict accounts, cancel or refuse transactions, or remove content where reasonably necessary for safety, fraud prevention, legal compliance or material breach, subject to applicable law.

10. Emergencies

For immediate danger, serious injury, suspected crime or fire, contact the appropriate emergency service first. GoStay is not an emergency-response service.

11. Legal review

These terms are a product-policy draft. They must be adapted to GoStay's final business structure, payment flow, insurance position, consumer obligations, privacy obligations and Botswana law before launch.

Private-room safety standard

GoStay's goal is to reduce avoidable risk while keeping the service welcoming.

Guest trust levels

  • Basic: account email confirmed.
  • Contact verified: email and phone confirmed.
  • Identity verified: a compliant identity-verification process has matched the submitted identity information.
  • Established guest: completed GoStay stays/reviews and no unresolved serious safety issues.

Host protections

  • Choose a minimum verification level for private-room listings.
  • Set maximum guests and visitor rules.
  • Publish quiet hours and prohibited conduct.
  • Keep valuables and private documents secured.
  • Use GoStay's incident/reporting route for disputes and suspicious activity.
What verification cannot say: identity verification does not prove a person has no criminal history, is not a thief, or will never behave recklessly. GoStay should communicate this clearly and avoid over-promising safety.

GoStay payment API architecture

Recommended flow

  1. Guest chooses a payment method.
  2. GoStay server creates a payment session with the approved provider.
  3. Guest completes payment on the provider's secure checkout/OTP flow.
  4. Provider sends a signed webhook/callback.
  5. GoStay verifies the callback server-side and marks the booking paid.
  6. GoStay records a transaction reference and reconciles settlement to the GoStay business account.
  7. Host payout occurs according to the final Host Agreement and applicable payment rules.

Payment methods to plan for

  • Cards: Visa and Mastercard should be a baseline; FNB Botswana also advertises acceptance of UnionPay, AMEX and Diners through its eCommerce gateway.
  • Orange Money: Orange provides a Botswana Web Payment/M Payment API for registered merchants.
  • MyZaka: Mascom provides MyZaka merchant payment services.
  • SMega / other wallet: include only after confirming the merchant/API arrangement.
  • Bank/EFT: useful as an alternative, with manual or automated reconciliation.
  • Gateway aggregator: evaluate a provider that legally supports the exact Botswana methods GoStay needs.

Do not put secret API keys in this HTML. Production keys belong on a secure backend/server environment. Never collect card CVV/PIN or mobile-money PIN in GoStay's own form.
